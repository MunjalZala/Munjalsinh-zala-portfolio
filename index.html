About

I'm Munjalsinh Zala, a Mechatronics Engineering student at SRH University Berlin (GPA 1.9 German Scale), with a hands-on focus on structural design, FEM simulation, and aerospace-grade testing.

As Mechanical Design Lead of Team Skybound at the CanSat Competition 2026, a NASA/NRL-sponsored rocket payload mission, I designed for real constraints: 30G ejection shock, tight mass budgets, and GPS-guided paraglider descent. We finished 14th of 36 international teams as the only German university team.

Beyond mechanical design, I build complete mechatronic systems: my teach-by-demonstration robot arm pairs two ESP32s over ESP-NOW with a filtered sensor pipeline, motion profiling, layered safety logic and a browser-based digital twin.

My certifications in SolidWorks and AutoCAD back up practical experience from an engineering internship and metalworking training at Handwerkskammer Potsdam. In March 2027 I join Quantum Systems as a Mechanical Hardware Design Intern, applying structural design and validation skills to sUAS / eVTOL development.
